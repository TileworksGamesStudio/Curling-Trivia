Guide

How to Play

1

Daily Release

A fresh 5-question trivia challenge unlocks every day at midnight UK time (Europe/London). Future daily releases remain locked.

2

One-Tap Delivery

Select the best answer from the four choices provided. Immediate tactile verification and verified explanations appear below each question.

3

Streaks & Scoring

Complete today's puzzle before the next midnight release to increment your daily streak. Missed days reset the streak to 1 upon completion.

4

The Vault

Catch up on any missed puzzles or practice with previous dates in the archive. Vault puzzles do not affect your active daily streak.
