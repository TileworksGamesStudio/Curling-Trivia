HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Each round presents five questions covering curling rules, tactics, and history. Answer correctly to score 100 points per question, up to a perfect 500 points.

DAILY CHALLENGE & ARCHIVE

  • Daily Challenge: A synchronized round available once each calendar day. Complete daily challenges on consecutive days to build your streak.
  • Archive: Access all available rounds at any time for unlimited practice and replay.

KEY CURLING TERMS

  • The House: The concentric scoring rings (12-foot, 8-foot, 4-foot, and button) at each end of the sheet.
  • The Button: The exact 1-foot center circle of the house.
  • The Hammer: The tactical advantage of throwing the final stone of an end.
  • The Hog Line: Stones must fully cross the far hog line without touching the sideline to remain in play.
  • Free Guard Zone (FGZ): Opponent stones positioned in front of the tee line outside the house cannot be removed until the 6th stone of an end.
  • Pebbling: Fine water droplets sprayed onto the ice that freeze into tiny domes, allowing the stone to glide and curl.
  • Broomstacking: The traditional post-game gathering where opponents share drinks and conversation.
